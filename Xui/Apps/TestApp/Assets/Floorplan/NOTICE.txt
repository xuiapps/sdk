Used in Floorplan in a Nutshell as the source/reference for the semantic vector
floorplan and reusable furniture models. Originally developed in Xui TestApp.

floor-plan.dwg and preview.webp have shorter local filenames; their bytes
are unchanged. See provenance.json for original filenames, URLs, and hashes.
The explorer (index.html), editor (editor.html), SVGs and scripts were authored
as part of Xui and moved into this repository. Retain LICENSE.md and NOTICE.
The SVG paths are interpreted reconstructions, not extracted DWG geometry.
The editor can display the unchanged WEBP as a reference overlay.

TestApp uses Office.svg, assembled from the standalone repository for its
Floorplan performance example. No DWG or raster is loaded at runtime.
